# Vietnamese beginner-book comparison protocol

Status: preregistration draft
Comparator availability checked: 2026-07-26
Applies to: the exact PDF and EPUB hashes frozen in the cohort manifest

## What this protocol can and cannot establish

This protocol tests bounded first-use outcomes for Vietnamese adults who are new to formal Buddhist meditation. It does not test awakening, spiritual authority, long-term clinical benefit, sales, literary prestige, or whether one Buddhist tradition is superior to another.

A favorable result may support only a claim such as:

> In a preregistered comparison among the named editions and tested novice population, this edition produced better first-use task performance while meeting the non-compensable safety gate.

It cannot support “the best meditation book”, “number one in Vietnam”, or “guaranteed to lead to Stream-entry”. If no book clearly wins, the report must say so.

## Fixed comparison panel

The panel represents three distinct beginner-facing positions in the current Vietnamese market. Each edition must be acquired lawfully and rechecked immediately before recruitment.

1. **Everyday Vietnamese mindfulness**
   - Thích Nhất Hạnh, *Thiền tập cho người bận rộn*, 14th reprint, Nhà xuất bản Thế Giới, 2024, 125 pages.
   - Evidence: [National Library of Vietnam, May 2024 bibliography](https://nlv.gov.vn/dmdocuments/tmqg-05-2024.pdf); [Phương Nam product record](https://nhasachphuongnam.com/products/thien-tap-cho-nguoi-ban-ron-tai-ban-nam-2024).

2. **Lay Vipassanā instruction**
   - Joseph Goldstein, Nguyễn Duy Nhiên trans., *Hướng dẫn thiền quán trong đời sống hằng ngày*, Nhà xuất bản Hà Nội, 2024, ISBN 978-604-442-422-4.
   - Evidence: [publisher product record](https://thaihabooks.com/products/huong-dan-thien-quan-trong-doi-song-hang-ngay-tai-ban-2024).

3. **Theravāda doctrine and practice from a monastic teacher**
   - Ayya Khema, Diệu Liên Lý Thu Linh trans., *Vô ngã vô ưu: Thiền quán về Phật đạo*, Nhà xuất bản Lao Động, 2024, ISBN 978-604-492-558-5.
   - Evidence: [National Library of Vietnam, March 2025 bibliography](https://nlv.gov.vn/dmdocuments/tmqg-03-2025.pdf); [publisher product record](https://thaihabooks.com/products/vo-nga-vo-uu-tai-ban-2024).

The panel is fixed before participant 1 starts. A title may be replaced only if it cannot be lawfully obtained. Replacement must use the same stratum, be documented before recruitment, and trigger a new preregistration version.

## Eligibility and exclusions

Participants must:

- be at least 18 years old;
- read Vietnamese comfortably;
- have no more than ten lifetime sessions of formal Buddhist meditation;
- have completed no prior cohort for this book;
- consent to the specified data handling;
- be able to use the assigned print, PDF, or EPUB format without assistance beyond the scripted orientation.

Exclude employees, contributors, translators, publishers, retailers, teachers, or close associates connected to any tested title. Record every screened person in a private screening ledger, including exclusions, before randomization.

## Study phases

### Phase A: diagnostic pilot

Use approximately 12 eligible participants per arm. This phase tests whether instructions, tasks, timing, and data capture work. It does not authorize a ranking claim. Freeze every change before moving to Phase B.

### Phase B: confirmatory comparison

Determine sample size by simulation using Phase A completion and task-success distributions. Publish the simulation code, assumed effect, type-I error control, attrition allowance, and stopping rule before the first confirmatory participant starts.

Randomize participants to titles and reading formats in advance. Use concealed allocation until enrollment is complete. The moderator follows a title-neutral script and does not disclose the project hypothesis. Outcome adjudicators receive de-identified answers without title, author, arm, or participant identity. The analyst receives arm labels A, B, C, and D until the statistical report is locked.

## Equivalent first-use task domains

Tasks must test the same reader problem, not identical page locations or title-specific trivia:

1. find a safe place to begin;
2. explain the first practice in their own words;
3. identify when to reduce, stop, or seek help;
4. distinguish direct experience from an interpretation;
5. respond to a difficult feeling without suppressing it or acting it out;
6. explain how ethics relates to practice;
7. identify what the book does not allow the reader to conclude;
8. return to a named section after closing or navigating away.

An independent panel must review task equivalence before recruitment. Title-specific doctrinal questions may be reported descriptively, but they cannot enter the comparative primary outcome unless every title explicitly teaches the tested construct.

## Outcome hierarchy

### Gate 1: safety, non-compensable

Any title fails the release comparison if:

- a participant follows its first-route instructions and is directed to persist through a stated medical or psychiatric danger sign;
- the title lacks a usable stop or help route for a task that explicitly presents escalating functional impairment or immediate danger;
- a serious distress event judged plausibly related to the study procedure occurs and the preregistered safety review does not clear continuation.

High comprehension, attractive design, or fast navigation cannot offset a safety failure.

### Gate 2: completion and abandonment

Report assignment-to-finish rate, time to first usable instruction, task completion, skipped tasks, withdrawals, and reasons. Analyze all randomized eligible participants in their assigned arm. Do not discard a difficult first session because the participant did not finish.

### Gate 3: beginner task success

The primary instructional measure is the proportion of participants who complete all core task domains without a disqualifying safety error. Report each domain separately as well as the preregistered all-domain measure. Answers are scored against frozen adjudication keys by two blinded raters; disagreements are resolved by a third rater.

### Gate 4: confidence calibration

Test whether readers can distinguish:

- source-backed teaching from editorial advice;
- a practice observation from a diagnosis;
- useful progress from proof of attainment;
- uncertainty from failure.

Overconfident false claims count as failures even when the answer contains relevant terminology.

### Gate 5: usability and accessibility

Report navigation time, return-to-section success, text enlargement or reflow success, visible focus/navigation problems, and format-specific abandonment. PDF and EPUB results remain separate. A structural EPUB validator pass is necessary but does not substitute for human reader-app use.

## Analysis and claim rule

- Publish arm-level denominators, missingness, withdrawals, exclusions, adverse events, and all preregistered outcomes.
- Adjust confirmatory pairwise comparisons for the three candidate-versus-comparator tests.
- Report absolute differences and uncertainty intervals, not only p-values.
- Do not merge safety into a weighted score.
- Do not change the primary measure, passing threshold, comparator panel, or exclusion rule after seeing arm outcomes.
- Preserve the possibility that different books serve different readers and that no single title is best.

The candidate may be described as outperforming the named editions only if it:

1. passes the safety gate;
2. is not meaningfully worse than every comparator on completion and abandonment under the preregistered margin;
3. shows confirmatory superiority to every comparator on the primary beginner task measure after multiplicity adjustment; and
4. does not show a meaningful deficit in confidence calibration or accessibility.

Otherwise, report the narrower result actually supported.

## Rights, privacy, and publication

- Use lawfully acquired complete editions. Do not scan, redistribute, or publish copyrighted comparator text.
- Do not “normalize” excerpts by rewriting competitors, because that tests the researchers' rewrite rather than the books.
- Store direct identifiers separately from response data. Public reports use coarsened environment fields and suppress small cells.
- Record a deletion deadline for raw participant records. Publish only aggregate results, frozen instruments, adjudication keys, analysis code, deviations, and artifact hashes.
- Register adverse events and withdrawals before unblinding the arm labels.

## Cheapest disconfirming test

Before funding a confirmatory study, run the diagnostic pilot and ask one hostile question: does the candidate actually help novices complete the same safety and practice tasks more reliably, or does its extra doctrinal depth merely increase reading burden? If the candidate does not clearly improve task success without increasing abandonment, stop the ranking project and revise the book.
